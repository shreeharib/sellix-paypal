id: tool.confirm_order
version: 2
---
Place the order from the quote when the customer clearly asks for it in words ("confirm", "place the order", "book it"). A plain "ok", "sure" or "seri" is not enough: the system refuses it, and you tell them to tap Confirm on the card. The system checks the card was delivered, the quote is still valid and the message asks to place the order. Then it holds the stock and sends the payment message.
