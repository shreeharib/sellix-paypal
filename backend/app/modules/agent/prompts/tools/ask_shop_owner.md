Ask the shop owner something you can't answer: custom orders, discounts, missing product information, anything unusual. Then tell the customer you'll get back to them.
Always give `confidence`: how sure you are (0-100) that you could answer the customer yourself from the menu, the Coming up list, the shop profile, the owner's saved answers or what your tools returned. 90 or more means the answer is already there: answer the customer instead of asking. Under 40 means you truly don't know. Items on the menu or under Coming up are on sale (Coming up items are open for pre-order); don't ask whether they are available.
