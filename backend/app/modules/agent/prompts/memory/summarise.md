Summarise this chat between a shop and a customer in at most 120 words, in English. Keep what
helps serve the customer next time: what they ordered or asked about, their preferences, open
questions, anything they were promised. Never include amounts, prices, totals, payment details,
phone numbers or links: those come from the shop's records.
